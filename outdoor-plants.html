Outdoor Plant Care

Help your garden
grow beautifully.

A few simple habits can make a big difference when caring for plants in outdoor spaces.

01

Choose the Right Sunlight

Place each plant where it receives the amount of sunlight it needs to grow well.

02

Water According to Need

Water deeply when needed and allow excess water to drain away from the roots.

03

Give Plants Room

Provide enough space between plants for healthy airflow and comfortable growth.

04

Prune & Refresh

Remove damaged leaves and overgrown branches to keep outdoor plants healthy.

A Little Reminder

A healthy garden
starts with
good attention.

Before watering, pruning or adding fertilizer, take a moment to observe your outdoor plants and understand what they need.

01

Check the Soil

Feel the soil before watering and make sure the planting area has enough drainage.

02

Watch the Weather

Adjust watering and plant care around heavy rain, strong sunlight and changes in outdoor conditions.

03

Look for New Growth

New leaves, flowers and healthy growth are useful signs that your plants are settling into their environment.
