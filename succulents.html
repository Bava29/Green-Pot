Succulent Care

Keep your succulents
healthy and thriving.

Succulents are easy to care for when you give them the right balance of sunlight, water and well-draining soil.

01

Give Plenty of Light

Place your succulent where it receives bright natural light for growth and color.

02

Water Sparingly

Let the soil dry between watering and avoid keeping the roots constantly wet.

03

Ensure Good Airflow

Keep enough space around your succulents so air can move freely in plants.

04

Trim When Needed

Remove dry or damaged leaves carefully to keep your succulent looking healthy.

A Little Reminder

Succulents
prefer less,
not more.

Too much water can be harder on a succulent than too little. Let the soil dry and give your plant time between watering.

01

Let the Soil Dry

Check the soil before watering and make sure the top layer has dried properly.

02

Avoid Standing Water

Always use a pot with proper drainage so excess water can escape easily.

03

Watch Their Shape

Stretching or leaning can indicate that your succulent needs more natural light.
