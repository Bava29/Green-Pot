Indoor Plant Care

Keep your plants
happy and healthy.

A few simple habits can make a big difference when caring for indoor greenery.

01

Find the Right Light

Place your plant where it receives the amount of natural light it needs.

02

Water Mindfully

Check the soil before watering and avoid keeping the roots constantly wet.

03

Give Them Space

Good airflow and enough room around your plants help them grow comfortably.

04

Trim & Refresh

Remove damaged leaves regularly to keep your plants looking fresh and healthy.

A Little Reminder

Good plant care
starts with
observation.

Before adding more water, sunlight or fertilizer, take a moment to look at what your plant is telling you.

01

Check the Soil

Touch the top layer of soil before watering. Dry soil usually means your plant is ready for a drink.

02

Rotate Your Plant

Turn your plant occasionally so different sides receive natural light and growth stays balanced.

03

Watch for Changes

Yellow leaves, dry edges or slow growth can be useful signals that your plant needs attention.
